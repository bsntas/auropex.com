05 Research & development

Native data first.

Languages like Nepali are spoken by millions yet barely present in the data that today's AI learns from. Good language technology starts with good text: carefully digitised, properly credited, gathered with the consent of the people who wrote it.

Pushtakalaya gives us that foundation. Our research turns it into tools that let local literature and culture travel to a global audience.

headingहिमालको कविता verseसाँझको घाम कञ्चनजङ्घामा
बादलको छायाँ बेंसीमा proseदार्जीलिङका पहाडहरूमा चियाका बगानहरू फैलिएका छन्, र बिहानको कुहिरोले सबैलाई ढाक्छ।
  • A

    Reading the printed page

    OCR for Nepali text, proofread against a growing word list, that measures its own accuracy page by page.

  • B

    Understanding layout

    Headings, columns, verse, interviews and speeches recognised on magazine and book pages, so text reads in the right order.

  • C

    Listening and looking up

    Read-aloud and dictionary tools that make Nepali books accessible to more readers.

  • D

    Toward language AI

    Corpora, benchmarks and models for Nepali and other languages of the region, built with rights holders, not around them.
